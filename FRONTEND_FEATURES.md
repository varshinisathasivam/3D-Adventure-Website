# Frontend Features — VERDANTIA

## Implemented Pages (All 14 Logical Experiences)

1. **Landing Page (`/`)**: Full-screen 3D animated planet hero with floating islands and clouds, GSAP text entrance, storytelling section, 4 world preview cards with live restoration progress, core features, and footer.
2. **Login & Register (`/auth`)**: Demo authentication with email/password validation, show/hide password toggle, confirm password check, error alerts, and `localStorage` persistence. *(Clearly labeled as frontend demo auth only.)*
3. **Avatar Creation (`/avatar`)**: Interactive 3D low-poly avatar preview with 4 character classes, 6 outfit colors, 4 skin tones, 4 hairstyles, hair colors, and 5 accessories. Saving locally unlocks the *Identity Forged* badge.
4. **3D World Explorer (`/explore`)**: Interactive 3D planet with 4 clickable zones, hover highlights, locked/unlocked states based on Explorer Level, live XP/Level bar, and world info cards.
5. **Green Forest World (`/world/forest`)**: Stylized 3D forest with growing trees, river, mountains, disappearing litter, returning wildlife, and 5 playable missions.
6. **Blue Ocean World (`/world/ocean`)**: Underwater 3D scene with schooling fish, coral reefs that regain color, removable surface plastic, and 5 playable missions.
7. **Desert World (`/world/desert`)**: Low-poly dunes, expanding oasis spring, emerging palm trees, and 5 water/solar conservation missions (unlocks at Level 2).
8. **Future City World (`/world/future-city`)**: Stylized eco-city with solar rooftops, spinning wind turbine, vertical gardens, and 5 sustainability missions (unlocks at Level 3).
9. **Mission Center (`/missions`)**: Unified view of all 20 missions with world, difficulty, and completion status filters plus reusable `MissionModal`.
10. **Local Eco-Guide Companion (`/companion`)**: Offline rule-based keyword chatbot covering 7 environmental topics with suggested questions and an honest fallback message.
11. **Player Dashboard (`/dashboard`)**: Live XP, level progress bar, completed missions count, combined restoration percentage, per-world progress bars, badge count, and recent activity feed.
12. **Rewards & Achievements (`/rewards`)**: 8-level progression ladder and 8 locked/unlocked achievement badges.
13. **Leaderboard (`/leaderboard`)**: Weekly and Overall rankings comparing clearly labeled sample demo explorers with the player's live local score.
14. **Profile & Settings (`/profile`)**: Username editor, graphics quality (`low` / `medium`), reduced-motion switch, Web Audio sound toggle, reset progress with confirmation, and logout.
